# Built-in asset library: commercial redistribution and native integration

Research date: 2026-10-09. This is the first feature-inventory milestone, not an implemented library. No assets, archives, fonts or models were downloaded in this milestone. The owner's authorization for future reviewed free commercial downloads persists; measured sizes, checksums, listening/visual quality and implementation acceptance remain pending. Lumeryn's original code and original template definitions remain proprietary. Third-party assets retain their own licenses.

## Recommended initial scope

Start with a curated CC0 sound-cue selection, four individually documented CC0 music-loop candidates, a small Lucide SVG selection with ISC/MIT notices, one unmodified OFL font family, and original Lumeryn editable title/diagram templates. Provide the same stable asset IDs and search results to Studio and external CLI agents. Avoid a mandatory cloud subscription, an embedded agent runtime, or a large default stock download.

Short interface sounds and jingles are useful for clicks, notifications, reveals and transitions. They are not a professional full-length music catalog. Music loops must be listened to, checked for seams and matched to narration before assigning mood/default-use labels. An author's seamless-loop claim is evidence to test, not a measured Lumeryn result.

## Provider classes that need different treatment

| Provider / terms | Individual project use | Raw bundled or downloadable Lumeryn stock library |
|---|---|---|
| [Freesound official FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/) | Filter by the individual sound's license. CC0 is simplest; CC BY can serve commercial projects with required credit. | Review each sound, author, exact license/version and provenance. Do not treat a collection/search filter as proof for every file. CC BY-NC is excluded from the commercial starter; retired Sampling+ and unclear files require individual review. |
| [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) | Commercial adaptation/redistribution is possible with attribution, license/source links, retained notices and change indication. | A future CC BY pack must preserve downstream rights and avoid EULA/DRM restrictions that obstruct them. Synchronizing licensed music with video is an adaptation under these terms. Ship asset notices and generate project/export credits; never relabel the raw asset proprietary. |
| [OpenGameArt FAQ](https://opengameart.org/node/5571) | Licenses vary by submission and can be offered as alternatives. | Default to individually verified CC0 submissions above. CC BY, OGA-BY, share-alike and GPL art are distinct cases; a site category or creator profile is not a blanket grant. OGA-BY is a separate license, not Creative Commons endorsement. |
| [Pixabay binding terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/) | Current Content License permits qualifying commercial creative combinations, with additional content/person/brand restrictions. | Exclude current Content License assets: standalone redistribution is prohibited, and resize/crop/filter alone does not make a new creative work. Terms identify pre-2019-01-09 published content as CC0, but any such exception needs item-specific evidence. |
| [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | Qualifying project use/modification is permitted without mandatory attribution, subject to content/endorsement restrictions. | Exclude from the starter stock catalog: redistribution on other stock/wallpaper platforms is prohibited. An exportable reusable media library should not assume its permission covers that use. A later integration needs appropriate provider terms. |
| [Microsoft Clipchamp stock terms](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/clipchamp/how-to-add-stock-music-and-audio-to-videos) | Appropriate plans permit commercial videos saved from Clipchamp using their stock. | Explicitly excluded: individual stock files cannot be redistributed, used in other editors, shared or sold. Do not extract Clipchamp media, templates or branding from its UI/cache. |
| [Current Remix Icon license](https://github.com/Remix-Design/RemixIcon/blob/master/License) | Custom Remix Icon License v1.0, January 2026, permits certain uses as part of larger products. | Do not assume older Apache/MIT descriptions are current. Standalone icon products and downloadable collections face restrictions; exclude from the reusable stock starter. Lucide has clearer ISC/MIT redistribution terms. |

Provider licenses do not change Lumeryn's proprietary status. An asset can remain CC0/OFL/ISC/MIT inside a commercial product without licensing original engine code under those terms. Retain attribution/rights information even when end users only see a simple commercial-use badge. Unknown rights must stay unknown rather than becoming `commercial_ok=true` by default.

## Original editable templates, not copied stock styles

Create original Lumeryn title, subtitle, lower-third, chapter, reveal, diagram and explainer presets. Proposed IDs such as `lumeryn.template.title.clean.v1` and `lumeryn.template.diagram.flow.v1` describe our definitions, not downloaded Clipchamp styles. Each template contains semantic Rust scene objects, parameters, safe-area/layout rules, theme/font references, animation curves and optional cue slots. Users and agents can edit text, timing, color and layout in the same project. A prerendered thumbnail is a preview, not the template's authoritative representation.

The screenshot galleries can inform a feature inventory and familiar actions. They do not authorize copying proprietary template definitions, preset names/artwork, media or branding. Asset-backed original templates list every external dependency/notice; procedural original shapes and synthesized original cues avoid third-party stock for basic diagrams. Do not claim semantic templates work before shared preview/export tests pass.

## Manifest, provenance and storage proposal

Use a versioned declarative pack manifest with immutable IDs. Record provider, pack revision/source commit, canonical source page/download URL, acquisition date, author, original filename, MIME/type, exact compressed/installed bytes, SHA-256 of archive and every installed file, existing BLAKE3 content identity, license SPDX/custom text and its hash, attribution, modifications, allowed raw redistribution, commercial-project permission, rights exceptions and review status. Use nullable checksums/sizes only in the research/catalog candidate state; an installed/approved pack must contain actual verified values. No checksum is invented in this document because bytes were not downloaded.

Stable aliases such as `sfx.ui.click.soft` can point to an immutable provider/file/version ID after actual selection. Localized names, tags, mood, duration and aliases may change without renaming the original disk file or changing IDs. A media rename command changes the project's display alias. Remove from project/library view means unlink that reference, not delete source media; referenced assets must be protected from catalog removal. Physical cleanup is a separate explicitly authorized operation.

Store pack bytes/notices under a per-user Lumeryn application-data directory, with immutable version directories and an indexed local SQLite catalog. Keep raw media/models/cache files on disk, not SQLite BLOBs. A small metadata manifest can ship with the minimal application; optional packs download once and remain usable offline. The global library is a new planned metadata feature, not an already-implemented global settings/model DB. Per-project SQLite and the authoritative editing service remain sufficient to reopen existing projects.

Use SQLite FTS5 for names/tags/description and ordinary indexes for type, installed state, license suitability, duration and pack ID. No vector model is needed for the first catalog. Bind literal user queries, paginate and return explicit missing/not-installed/unsupported states. Audio duration uses integer samples and sample rate; video uses rational frame rate; beat/loop markers are measured metadata, not floating-point editing authority.

Downloading a pack streams to a private temporary path with byte limits, validates a trusted pinned manifest/hash, rejects archive traversal/absolute paths/symlinks and oversized extraction, then publishes a complete new version atomically. Provider metadata can change; a hash computed locally after first retrieval identifies bytes but does not independently prove authenticity. Preserve the approved source/license record and pin it for subsequent distribution. Do not execute scripts/plugins embedded in a pack. SVGs must reject executable/external-resource payloads before native rendering; fonts/media are probed in bounded workers.

Imported library items become ordinary stable project media IDs through the service, preserving source identity and revision checks. For portable projects, copy approved redistributable assets and their notices into a new project-owned assets directory, or expose relinking if external references are retained. Existing `project backup` preserves references and is not a media-packaging feature. Export/package code must distinguish raw file redistribution from inclusion in a rendered video.

## Native/CLI/service integration proposal — not shipped commands

The external agent discovers capabilities, lists/searches the catalog, inspects the chosen asset's license/actual analysis, auditions it, then submits a revision-aware placement operation. Studio uses the same catalog/service API for searchable tabs, preview, favorites and insert actions. It never owns a separate editing interpretation or invokes an embedded agent.

| Proposed operation | Contract |
|---|---|
| `assets packs list`, `assets search`, `assets inspect` | Local machine-readable metadata, stable IDs, bounded pagination and truthful installed/rights/analysis status. |
| `assets packs install` | Background optional-download job with cancellation, limits, pinned hash and atomic install; no unexpected subscription or paid acquisition. |
| `assets preview` | One bounded audition/thumbnail request, gain applied to monitoring only, no graph mutation or source rewrite. |
| `assets import` / existing media registration | Resolve a reviewed installed item and register the same source in the chosen project. Any mutation uses expected revision, optional idempotency and batch validation. |
| `templates list`, `templates inspect`, `templates instantiate` | Discover original editable template schema/parameters/dependencies and commit semantic scene objects through Rust service commands when supported. |
| `project credits` / package manifest | Report required attributions/licenses for actually used assets and include notices when raw files are packaged. |

Standalone music/SFX placement requires real audio-track, gain/fade/loop/mix and shared preview/export semantics; the current single-video-track/embedded-audio baseline does not already provide those capabilities. A catalog UI must disable unsupported placement and explain it. Dry-run/atomic batches and exact timing remain necessary when inserting an asset or template; a successful download is not a successful edit.

## Installed size, scheduling and verification targets

Proposed starter budget: at most 64 MiB of curated installed assets, with exact bytes reported before shipping. Retain compact source audio; do not eagerly expand every sound to PCM. Larger full Kenney/retro/music packs and language fonts are optional. Provider-reported compressed sizes above are planning evidence, not measured installed sizes or quotas already enforced.

Proposed library UI budget: metadata-only search, paginated 32-item grids, lazy visible thumbnails and one active audio audition. For scale intuition, 32 RGBA 128×128 thumbnails occupy 2 MiB before renderer overhead; ten seconds of 48 kHz stereo float PCM is 3,840,000 bytes before other buffers. These are arithmetic budgets, not measured process/VRAM results. Reuse bounded native decode/cache workers and resource admission; prioritize playback over background indexing/downloads. Asset font ingestion, substantial scene layout and SVG/audio decoding belong in bounded background workers; ordinary egui text painting keeps its native lifecycle. Avoid a second renderer/backend merely to browse assets.

Implementation acceptance must measure installed bytes, search p50/p95, cold/warm preview latency, memory/CPU/available GPU telemetry, waveform/thumbnail correctness, loop seams and narration intelligibility on actual assets. Verify offline reopen, missing packs, interrupted installs, checksum mismatch, traversal/oversize rejection, no overwrite/source mutation, stable IDs after aliases, and shared agent/Studio insert revisions. Test decoded render/preview parity for real audio placement and editable scene templates. No current quality/performance pass is claimed.

## Recording, TTS and transcription remain separate optional capabilities

Local microphone/screen/camera recording creates a new user-selected file with explicit device/start/stop/cancel behavior, bounded workers and retained timing metadata. Register it through the same service after capture; never overwrite original sources. Device availability, capture permission, timing/drift and recording interruption need platform testing. A user's recording is not a pre-cleared public stock asset.

- Transcription candidate: [Whisper's official README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/whisper/main/README.md) explicitly licenses both its code and original model weights under MIT. [whisper.cpp's MIT license](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE) is a native runtime candidate. Audit pinned runtime/build dependencies and exact model files, include notices, keep downloads optional and benchmark memory/language accuracy. Third-party fine-tunes do not inherit permission solely from a Whisper name.
- TTS candidate: [Kokoro-82M's official model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/raw/main/README.md) declares Apache-2.0 weights, describes voices/training attribution, and publishes v1.0 model SHA-256 `496dba118d1a58f5f3db2efc88dbdc216e0483fc89fe6e47ee1f2c53f18ad1e4`. That is provider-published evidence, not a locally verified download. Audit the exact runtime, voice files, phonemizer/dependencies, notices and output terms independently. The default instructions install eSpeak-NG; its [GPLv3 license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/master/COPYING) needs a separate distribution/linking review.
- [Current Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) is GPL-3.0 and embeds eSpeak-NG. It is not a permissive drop-in for the proprietary engine. A separately installed subprocess could be investigated with exact GPL distribution obligations; this research does not approve linking/bundling it. Voice/model licenses are separate: the [Lessac voice model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/lessac/medium/MODEL_CARD) points to dataset-specific terms, despite the repository's broader MIT badge.

Never equate a runtime license, model-repository badge or stock-library license with every voice's commercial permission. Pin model/voice files, review their model cards and dependency/output terms, retain provenance and enforce hardware-aware optional jobs. Automatic captions require measured recognition and editable text/timing; TTS does not need an embedded LLM/agent runtime. No runtime/model installation or paid API call occurred in this milestone.
